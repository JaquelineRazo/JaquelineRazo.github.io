Point of View

Building at the intersection of business and technology.

I've worked across global organizations, technology teams, startups and independent engagements in Mexico, the United States, Europe and Türkiye, usually where the problem is complex, the answer isn't obvious and several teams need to move in the same direction.

My approach is simple: understand the problem, structure the path forward and build what makes the business work better, combining business context, data, technology and conversations with the people closest to the work.

Strategy means very little without execution.

That's why I started RAZZO. Most businesses don't need more tools. They need the ones they already have to work as one system.
