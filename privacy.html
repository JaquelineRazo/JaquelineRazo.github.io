Privacy
01

Who is responsible

Jaqueline Razo, an individual operating under the RAZZO brand, based in Mexico City, Mexico. For anything related to your information, write to jaqueline@razzobusiness.com.

02

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me:

  • Through the contact form: your name, email, company (optional), the topic you select, your message, and how you found the site (for example, a campaign tag in the link).
  • By email: whatever you include in your message.
  • If you book a call: the details the booking tool asks for, such as your name, email and what you would like to talk about.

The hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) to operate and secure the site.

03

Why I use it

To reply to you, follow up on the conversation you started and keep a record of it. I do not sell your information, and I will not add you to any marketing list without your explicit permission.

04

Who processes it for me

These providers store or transmit information on my behalf:

  • FormSubmit, which delivers the contact form to my inbox.
  • Google Workspace, for email and calendar.
  • TidyCal, for booking calls.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this site.

Several of them are located in the United States, so your information may be transferred and stored outside your country.

05

How long I keep it

For as long as our conversation or working relationship lasts, and up to 24 months after our last contact. After that, I delete it.

06

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used, by writing to jaqueline@razzobusiness.com. I will respond as soon as possible and within the time required by applicable law.

07

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, this notice will be updated first.

08

Changes

Any change to this notice will be published on this page with a new date.
