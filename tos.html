Terms of Service

This internal application connects the TikTok Marketing API with Mars Petcare’s analytics environment.

It is used exclusively by authorized personnel from Mars Pet Nutrition Data Driven Marketing (DDM) to retrieve aggregated advertising performance metrics (impressions, reach, clicks, views, engagement, spend) for internal dashboards and performance reporting.

The tool does not collect personal user data or interact with TikTok users. All usage complies with Mars’ internal data governance and privacy standards.
